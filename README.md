# 🧵 Strings

A simple social media app to share your thoughts, connect with friends, and discover interesting content.

## ✨ Features

- 📝 **Create Posts** - Share your thoughts with text and images
- ❤️ **Like Posts** - Show love to content you enjoy
- 💬 **Reply** - Join conversations with comments
- 🔁 **Repost** - Share cool content with your followers
- 🔖 **Save Posts** - Bookmark content for later
- 👥 **Follow Users** - Build your network and customize your feed
- 🔍 **Search** - Find and discover other users
- 👤 **Profiles** - View posts, replies, and reposts from anyone
- ⚙️ **Settings** - Dark mode, edit profile, and more

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Sign Up
1. Go to the app
2. Click **Sign up**
3. Enter your name, username, and password
4. Done! You're ready to post

### Sign In
1. Click **Sign in**
2. Enter your username and password
3. Access your feed and start exploring

## 🎯 Quick Tips

- Follow users to see their posts in your feed
- Click on usernames to visit their profile
- Like and reply to engage with the community
- Save posts you want to read later
- Use dark mode from the menu for a comfortable experience

## ❓ Questions?

- **Can I delete posts?** - Yes, delete your own posts anytime
- **Can I edit posts?** - No, but you can delete and repost
- **Who can see my posts?** - Everyone! Posts are public
- **How do I change my username?** - Contact support
- **Is my data safe?** - Yes, passwords are encrypted and secure
